Ensure the Quality of Your Software with Rawlson

Rawlson Tech. is a global IT consulting and software development company providing complete IT Solutions. It has built strong partnerships with key software testing vendors and built its own test tools and frameworks to support continuous testing and quality engineering.

Our proactive testing experts can dive into your project within 1-3 days and thoroughly validate every aspect of your software. With our team handling quality assurance, you can focus on building core business value while we ensure bug-free, seamless releases.

To support our aim of achieving greater market acceptance for all our projects, we use both commercial and open-source test tools along with continuous integration pipelines to minimize testing time and maximize coverage.
